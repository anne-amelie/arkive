# Arkive 

> A journal for your entertainment journey.

Arkive is a personal media tracker designed to keep track of movies, series and episodes.

## Features

- 📚 Track watched shows and movies
- 🎬 Track episodes progress
- 🔍 Search and organize your collection

## Why Arkive?

Arkive turns your watch history into a collection of stories you've discovered.
